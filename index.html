Research

Research Interests: Corporate Finance, Corporate Governance, Labor and Finance, Innovation

My research examines how regulation and institutional frictions shape firms' investment, innovation, and strategic decisions.

My current research studies how tax incentives, labor-market regulation, intellectual property protection, and information about acquisition quality affect corporate investment and strategic decisions.
